# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students in Pakistan looking for hostel accommodation, and hostel owners who list their properties.

## Product Purpose

HostelLo helps students discover and book hostels while giving owners a place to list their properties.

## Positioning

HostelLo is a student-first hostel marketplace. The product opens directly to hostel listings rather than a marketing landing page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Students can browse hostel listings and optionally filter discovery by city.
- Email/password sign-in is implemented. Google sign-in is available only when its provider credentials are configured.
- Phone OTP endpoints verify phone numbers for signed-in users; they do not provide phone-based sign-in.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: HostelLo.
- Keep the listings-first product experience; do not introduce a marketing homepage.

## Product Principles

- Put hostel discovery before promotion.
- Keep student needs central to the browsing and account experience.
- Make optional search filters clear about what they do.
- Show only authentication methods supported by the configured product.
